The old site was framed as a "VC investment platform" — venture capital fund math, IRR calculators, exit modelers. None of that fit what Leaving The Matrix actually is, which is a community of stock traders, options traders, ETF accumulators, and a few brave souls in the monthly-options-gamble channel.

So we rebuilt it from scratch.

## What's different

- **Built on Astro** instead of SvelteKit. We get near-instant page loads on mobile and a content-first architecture that makes the blog the centerpiece, not an afterthought.
- **One brand, four floors.** The home page reflects the actual Discord shape: education, signals, community, and the pro floor. No more pretending we're a venture-capital tracker.
- **Real legal posture.** Disclaimer page, terms, privacy — placeholders pending lawyer review, but they exist now. Trading-signal services without disclosures are asking for trouble.
- **Pricing on this page is a placeholder.** Final tier numbers replace the "TBD" values shortly.

## What's coming

- The full Nova dev blog backfilled here — every post we've published on unycross.com about building the trading copilot that powers our signals.
- Market notes, trade reviews, and education posts as their own categories.
- A real about page once Luke writes it.
- Final pricing.

For now, the door's open. Drop into `#getting-started` and say hi.
